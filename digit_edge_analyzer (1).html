How the score works

Bayesian smoothing (Jeffreys prior) reduces overreaction to small samples. Recent frequency is compared with the 10% equal-digit baseline; streaks and a chi-square uniformity check are shown as context. The highest estimated digit is only a candidate, not a reliable next-tick forecast. No martingale, auto-trading, or account credentials.
